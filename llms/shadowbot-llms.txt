## What players can do

- Play original word, logic, arcade, card, board, memory, and multiplayer games.
- Explore a licensed HTML5 catalog from external game providers.
- Complete daily challenges and optional account-based rewards.
- Use the site on modern desktop, mobile, and tablet browsers.
